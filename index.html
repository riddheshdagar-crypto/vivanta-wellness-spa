Our Speciality

Vivanta Ayurvedic Hair Treatment

Experience the ancient healing power of Ayurvedic hair treatment, a three-step therapeutic journey designed to transform your hair and restore your vitality.

01

Shiroabhyanga

A luxurious oil massage of the scalp, neck, and shoulders. Warm herbal oils are gently massaged to stimulate circulation, nourish hair roots, and release tension from the head and neck.

Relieves Tension Nourishes Roots Improves Circulation
→
02

Shirolepa

A therapeutic herbal paste is applied to the scalp and left for 20-30 minutes. This specially formulated blend of Ayurvedic herbs cools, heals, and detoxifies the scalp while conditioning the hair.

Deep Cleansing Scalp Healing Hair Conditioning
→
03

Banana Leaf Wrap

The scalp is gently wrapped with fresh banana leaves, creating a cooling and therapeutic environment. This ancient technique locks in the benefits of the herbal paste and provides deep nourishment.

Cooling Effect Deep Nourishment Natural Healing

Our Approach

Ayurvedic Philosophy

At Vivanta, we believe in treating the root cause, not just symptoms. Our approach is grounded in the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda, which recognizes that true wellness comes from balancing the body, mind, and spirit.

Each guest receives a personalized consultation to understand their unique constitution (prakriti) and current imbalances (vikriti). We then tailor our therapies to your specific needs, ensuring maximum efficacy and lasting results.

Holistic Healing

We treat the whole person, not isolated symptoms.

Natural Remedies

Expert Guidance
